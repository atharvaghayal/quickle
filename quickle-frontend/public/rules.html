🎮 QUICKLE – RULES CARD

A daily 5-letter guessing challenge with twists & timing!

🕹️ HOW TO PLAY

  • Guess the 5-letter hidden word within 6 total attempts.
  • Each guess gives you feedback to help you move closer to the solution.
  • Letter with 🟢 color indicates letter in correct place.
  • Letter with 🟡 color indicates letter exists in word but at wrong place.
  • Letter with ⚫ color indicates that letter dosen't exists in the word.
  • Fewer guesses = higher points!

🏅 POINTS SYSTEM (Attempts 1–5)

Guess Number Points Earned
1st Guess25 pts
2nd Guess18 pts
3rd Guess15 pts
4th Guess12 pts
5th Guess6 pts

⏱️ THE FINAL TWIST – TIMED 6th GUESS

If you still haven’t cracked the word after 5 tries, you get one last chance under strict time pressure!

⏳ 0–5 sec → 5 pts 💨

⏳ 6–9 sec → 3 pts

⏳ 10–12 sec → 1 pt 🐢

⏳ >12 sec → 0 pts ❌

❗ IF YOU FAIL ALL 6 GUESSES, "LIFE IS UNFAIR"

A small penalty is added to you score.

➤ Point Deduction: –5 points 🔻

If your score is already 0 and you fail to guess, your score becomes –5 points.
